About Lead Law

Lead Law is an Indian law firm with a practice focused on infrastructure, project finance and strategic investment. The firm advises lenders, investors, developers, corporates and public sector institutions on transactions, projects and disputes that shape infrastructure development and capital deployment across India.

Recognised among Infralogic's Top 10 firms in Asia for PPP Project Finance for four consecutive years, Lead Law has developed particular strength in advising on mandates where financing structures, regulatory frameworks and commercial execution intersect. The firm's experience spans complex financing transactions, infrastructure projects, corporate investments, capital markets and dispute resolution, providing clients with coordinated legal support across the lifecycle of significant commercial mandates.

Beyond its legal practice, Lead Law has established a multidisciplinary advisory platform integrating legal, financial and investment expertise. This approach enables the firm to advise on matters requiring a combination of legal analysis, financial evaluation and commercial judgement, particularly in infrastructure, project finance and strategic investments.

With offices across India's principal commercial centers and an established network of associate lawyers in 16 states, the firm advises clients on domestic and cross-border matters while maintaining a consistent standard of service across jurisdictions. Lead Law's clients include financial institutions, infrastructure developers, corporates, investors, family offices and public sector entities. The firm also works alongside government authorities and regulatory institutions on matters involving policy development, infrastructure implementation and strategic transactions.
